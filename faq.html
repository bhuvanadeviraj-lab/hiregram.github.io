Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using Hiregramm.

🔍 General

What is Hiregramm?

+

Hiregramm is a modern job platform connecting professionals with top companies. Job seekers can find opportunities across industries, and employers can post jobs and discover qualified candidates.

Is Hiregramm free for job seekers?

+

Yes, absolutely! Creating a profile, searching jobs, and applying are completely free for job seekers.

How do I create an account?

+

Click the "Log in" button in the top right and choose "Sign up". You can register with your email or via Google/LinkedIn. It takes less than two minutes.

👤 For Job Seekers

How do I apply for a job?

+

Browse jobs, click on a job card to see details, then click "Apply Now". You'll be guided to submit your resume and a cover letter. You can track your applications in your dashboard.

Can I save jobs to apply later?

+

Yes! Use the bookmark icon on any job listing to save it. Saved jobs can be accessed from your profile.

How do I know if my application was received?

+

After applying, you'll receive a confirmation email. You can also check the status in your "Applied Jobs" dashboard.

🏢 For Employers

How do I post a job?

+

Click "Post a Job" in the header. Fill in the job details, company info, and choose a posting plan. Once published, your job will be visible to thousands of job seekers.

How much does it cost to post a job?

+

We offer flexible plans starting from $199 for a 30-day listing. Volume discounts are available for multiple postings. Check our Pricing page for details.

Can I edit a job after it's posted?

+

Yes, you can edit job details from your employer dashboard. Changes will be reflected immediately.

⚙️ Account & Billing

How do I reset my password?

+

On the login page, click "Forgot password". Enter your email and we'll send you a reset link.

How do I delete my account?

+

Go to your account settings and select "Delete account". This action is permanent and removes all your data from Hiregramm.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal. Invoices are available upon request.

Still have questions? Contact our support team – we're happy to help.
